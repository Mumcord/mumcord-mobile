# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Manual device checklist for every release: what to try on real phones and headsets, with battery and data measurements
- Android and iOS: the Mumcord app icon (the desktop icon) on the home screen, with a themed icon on Android 13 and later, and the Mumcord glyph in Android notifications
- Repository skeleton: directory layout, Rust workspace, pinned engine reference, contributor rules
- Bridge between the Mumcord engine and the apps: connect, certificate question, disconnect, live view of channels and people
- Android app: connects to a Mumble server, asks before trusting an unknown or changed server certificate, shows the channel tree with people, disconnects
- Android app: Channels tab with collapsible channel tree, talking and state icons, join by tap, channel passwords, person sheet (volume and mute for you), search, and the bottom tabs with Disconnect under More
- Android: secrets are kept encrypted with a key in the phone's secure hardware and are excluded from backups
- iOS app project with Keychain storage (this device only)
- Android: Mumcord look in dark and light (colours, fonts, icons and base components shared with the desktop app), following the system setting and the system font size
- Bridge: saved servers, server ping, connect by saved server, channel passwords, per-person volume and mute, talking and stream state, `mumble://` link parsing, talk mode setting
- Android: first-start talk mode choice, server list with people online and latency, add, edit and remove servers, certificate warnings, connection errors with retry, one server at a time, and `mumble://` links that connect only after confirmation
- Android: connecting to a server on your local network explains why access to nearby devices is needed before Android asks for it (Android 17 and later); if it is refused, the error says so and leads to the system settings
- iOS: first-start talk mode choice, server list, add, edit and remove servers, certificate warnings, connection errors with retry, `mumble://` links with confirmation, and the Channels tab with channel tree, join, channel passwords, person sheet, search and Disconnect under More – the same as on Android
- Bridge: voice controls (push-to-talk, mute, deafen, whisper and shout, level meter, noise suppression and gain switches) and a real-time audio path for the apps' microphone and speaker
- Android: voice is audible. The phone's speaker and microphone run in voice-call mode with the system's echo cancellation; the microphone is asked for when you can first talk, with a one-sentence reason, and listening works without it
- Android: voice bar on every tab (talk indicator, mute, deafen, leave) and the full voice view with the people in your channel, the talk mode switch, hold-to-talk or the microphone level, and whisper to a person or shout to a channel with a hold button
- iOS: voice is audible, with the system's echo cancellation; the microphone is asked for when you can first talk, with a one-sentence reason. Voice bar, full voice view, hold-to-talk, mute, deafen, talk mode switch, whisper and shout as on Android. Before the first connection to a server on your local network the app explains why iOS will ask for access; if such a server cannot be reached, the error says so and leads to the system settings
- Bridge: channel chat, private messages, history, images, GIF favourites, mentions, replies, message requests and blocking, with server content delivered only as a safe structured form
- Android: a voice session keeps running in the background and with a locked screen, is shown to the phone as a call with a notification for Mute, Deafen and Leave (never in the recent calls), mutes and deafens during a phone call and restores your state afterwards, and tells you at the next start if the system closed the app while you were connected
- Android app: Chat tab with the chat of your current channel – messages with links, inline images and GIFs, replies and highlighted mentions, history kept on the phone, sending text with `@` autocomplete, pictures from the photo library or camera, and GIF favourites
- iOS: Chat tab as on Android – the chat of your current channel with links, inline images and GIFs, replies and highlighted mentions, sending text with `@` autocomplete, pictures from the photo library or camera, and GIF favourites
- iOS: voice keeps running in the background and with a locked screen and is shown to the system as an ongoing call (mute and end on the call screen, not listed in recent calls); accepting a phone call mutes and deafens you until it ends; after the system closed the app during a session, the next start says so and offers to reconnect
- Android: Messages tab with private conversations, message requests, starting a conversation and chat requests, blocking with a list of blocked people, and system notifications for private messages and mentions while connected, with "Show sender only"
- iOS: Messages tab with private conversations, message requests, starting a conversation and chat requests, blocking with a list of blocked people, and local notifications for private messages and mentions while connected, with "Show sender only"
- Android: the person sheet offers "Unblock" for everyone you blocked, also on an earlier connection, and "Send chat request" only for people whose app understands it; "Show sender only" is kept with the other settings of the app
- iOS: audio output – a connected wired or Bluetooth headset is used automatically, the first connection without one asks "Loudspeaker or earpiece?" on an iPhone and remembers the answer, and the voice view shows the output and switches between all of them; muting from a headset or a car (such as the stem of AirPods) mutes or unmutes the microphone with a short sound, while the system's End button always leaves the session
- iOS: the app gets back into the same channel with the same mute state when the phone changes between Wi-Fi and mobile data; Settings (under More) with the mobile data switches "Start streams automatically", "Load images and GIFs" and "Reduced voice quality" (24 kbit/s on a metered network) and "Disconnect when alone for 30 minutes", which warns with a notification that has a "Stay connected" button
- Android: audio output – a connected wired or Bluetooth headset is used automatically, the first connection without one asks "Loudspeaker or earpiece?" and remembers the answer, and the voice view shows the output and switches between all of them; one press of a headset's button mutes or unmutes the microphone with a short sound, also with the screen off
- Android: the app gets back into the same channel with the same mute state when the phone changes between Wi-Fi and mobile data; Settings (under More) with the mobile data switches "Start streams automatically", "Load images and GIFs" and "Reduced voice quality" (24 kbit/s on a metered network) and "Disconnect when alone for 30 minutes", which warns with a notification and a "Stay connected" button one minute before
- Android: identities – the first start asks for an identity before anything else (scan from the desktop app, import a `.p12` file or create a new one); More → Identities lists them with their fingerprint and lets you make one the default, export a password-protected backup through the share sheet and delete one after typing its label (never the last). "Scan from desktop app" reads the QR codes of the desktop's "Send to your phone" in any order with progress, takes the 8-digit code with three attempts, shows what arrives and imports the identities and servers only after you confirm (server passwords are never transferred); the camera is asked for in context, and taking a photo for the chat now asks for it the same way
- Android: "Browse public servers" under "Find more" on the server list – the public Mumble server list, loaded only when you open it, with search by name, address or country, a country filter, people online and latency for the servers on screen, adding a server to your list and connecting to it; a failed refresh keeps the list loaded before
- Bridge: own comment and avatar, moderation (move, server mute and deafen, kick, ban) as far as the server allows it, the activity log of the connection, and the language, chat and notification settings
